When a project chooses which language models to use, the agent advising it is itself one vendor's model, and a vendor-specific API skill may load and supply that vendor's prices, features and defaults. Its framing then drifts toward that vendor: comparisons drawn only within one vendor's line-up, other options deferred, and recalled terms (e.g. data retention) presented as fact. The owner had to call it out.

What worked: research the options from sources with a brief that names no vendor and asks for even-handedness; have the design say models are interchangeable behind a port and chosen per task by measurement; and put several vendors, open-weight and closed, on the shortlist the measurement chooses from.
