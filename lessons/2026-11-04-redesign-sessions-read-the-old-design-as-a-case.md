In an open session that redraws a design, the agent grounds itself by reading the current system design and code, and then raises tensions and questions framed in that design's mechanisms, which the session is replacing. Across two consecutive sessions in one Adopter, most of the agent's raised tensions were leftovers the owner then had to dismantle: a better cutter re-cutting material the new design cuts once, re-running a judgement after a regrouping that makes no new work, a grouping key carried from the old unit of work, a coined term ("Story Event") for something the vocabulary already named. Each cost a turn and left nothing. The Skill already says that cases produced under the flaws being addressed show where a concept is missing, not what it should be; it should say the same of the design being replaced: read it for what exists, but before raising a tension from it, check that the tension survives the concepts already settled in the session, and drop it unasked if it only exists in the old shape.
