A design recalled that linking one vendor product to another (an ad network to an analytics product) would put each ad impression's revenue into the analytics' raw export. Research found that the linked event feeds the vendor's own reports but is excluded from the raw export, which the vendor lists in a table of automatic events. Integration pages describe what a link does in the vendor's dashboards, and recall generalises that to the raw data. Wherever a design reads a vendor's raw data, the claim should be checked against the export's schema and its list of excluded fields, not against the integration's page.
