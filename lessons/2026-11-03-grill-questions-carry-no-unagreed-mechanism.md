A grill question that carries a mechanism the owner never proposed inside its framing (a cap on how much of something is shown, a trigger such as "when an item arrives") costs several turns: the owner has to stop and dig the mechanism out before the question can be answered, and an earlier "yes" to the question can read as agreement to it. In one session, an implementation-level size limit slipped into a design question, and a question assumed work happens only when material arrives, against the design's own stance that commands run over the record at any time. The Skill should say: before asking, check that every mechanism a question presumes was either stated by the owner or is in the docs; anything else is offered on its own as the agent's proposal, never folded into another question; and sizing or performance concerns are left to the slice that can measure them, not raised in a design question.
