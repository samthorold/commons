Portugal's tax portal (Portal das Finanças) serves outdated consolidated codes beside current ones, with nothing on the page to say which is which: one corporate-tax code path alongside another, and a VAT code page still showing an article in its 2006 wording. A researcher who lands on the stale copy cites superseded law. Briefs for Portuguese tax research should name the current path, or ask the researcher to check each article's version against the official gazette (Diário da República).
