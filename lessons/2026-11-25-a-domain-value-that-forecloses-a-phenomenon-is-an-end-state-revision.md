Setting a substrate mechanism's value from the domain, within its bound, can close a pathway that other hypotheses of the end state depend on. Here the domain's value for living tissue's resistance to unadapted consumers is total, and the model's consumers carry no adaptation, so no world had consumers of living tissue. That foreclosed a role hypothesis and a phenomenon's setup, and it then made a later mechanism undecidable: its domain cost belongs to prey capture, and prey capture was gone. A substrate slice is a local call, but one whose value forecloses what another hypothesis needs is a revision of the end state, so it goes to the owner before the slices that depend on it are judged. The check before briefing such slices: does any slice's domain evidence rest on a pathway another slice may remove? If so, sequence them, and stop at the one that removes it.
