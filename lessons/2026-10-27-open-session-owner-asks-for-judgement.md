The open-session Skill warns against confident recommendations, because a question gets reshaped around the agent's candidate. Midway through a session the owner asked outright for the agent's judgement "to help move the session forward". A whole judgement worked: one argued position, its evidence, and where it was weakest, ending in a single candidate wording. The owner settled the crux from it. But the owner accepted it loosely ("a lot of the judgement makes sense"), the agent re-asked about the candidate twice, and the owner had to point out it was already settled.

How it would apply: the Skill says that when the owner asks for judgement, the agent gives it whole, as above, and that a loose acceptance of a judgement whose centre is one candidate settles that candidate. The agent records it as settled and plays it back, rather than asking again.
