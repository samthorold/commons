An emergent system can accumulate mechanisms that are "implemented, off by default, until a reading recommends it". Each is designed from domain sources, then kept behind a switch and judged by whether it makes a target phenomenon pass. That is adoption by result, the local optimisation the end-state Stance warns against, and it hides Drift: readings stack switch on switch while the physics that actually runs never changes. The substrate should be one physics. A mechanism the domain grounds is in, and runs in every world, with its rule as a substrate hypothesis. One the domain doesn't ground is not in the code, or stays as a stated simplification. A switch exists only to set up a controlled variant that a hypothesis reads in deliberately.
