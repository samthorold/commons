An act whose output is opinion can't have its model chosen by measuring properties on fixtures, as the Stance has it. Properties can only say the citations are valid and the checks pass, which nearly any capable model meets, and the difference that matters between models is whether the opinion is worth reading. The guide's only route for that is the reading in use (tier 3), and it doesn't say this reading can be what chooses the model. So the owner reading samples, or the output in use, may be the measurement for such an act, while the agent's own preference for its vendor stays out by leaving the choice to the owner. An adopter hit this and recorded it as an exception in its system design.
