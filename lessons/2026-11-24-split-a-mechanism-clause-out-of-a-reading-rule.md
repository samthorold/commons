A reading rule that joins an outcome ("the candidate plays stronger") and the mechanism the hypothesis credits for it ("…by doing X more often") with *and* puts one cell in *undecided* that more readings can't move: the outcome holds and the mechanism doesn't. That cell is a finding (the part matters, but not for the reason given), yet the rule sends it back to be read again forever. Before the first reading, split the mechanism into a finer hypothesis under the parent. The parent is read on the outcome alone. The child is read on the mechanism and is undecided only while the parent isn't supported. Then every cell reads as something. A related point: an instrument the candidate itself would judge (its own view of whether it did X) changes along with what it measures, so the child's instrument is the incumbent's judgement, with the candidate's own view reported but not ruled on.
