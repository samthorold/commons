When a domain claim is corrected to match its source, the evidence-quality note that recorded the disagreement invites a history line ("the text had said X; corrected in #N"). That is the chronology the no-decision-records Stance forbids, in a domain doc. The note should say, in the present tense, that the text follows the source and how the source was read; what the text said before is in git. The Guide says how a note records a disagreement, but not how it reads once the disagreement is resolved.
