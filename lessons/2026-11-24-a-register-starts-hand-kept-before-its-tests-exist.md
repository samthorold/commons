An Adopter adopting the end-state Stance on an existing design starts with a register kept by hand, recorded as a Deviation: the register is meant to be derived from tests and readings, but at adoption most hypotheses have no test yet ("no test yet" as their deferral reason) and the readings that exist predate the reading rules. A derived register has nothing to derive from until the tests are written, so every Adopter that adopts the Stance mid-life will hit this. The Guide could say that a register starts hand-kept at adoption, with derivation as the condition to revisit once enough hypotheses have tests, so the Deviation isn't recorded afresh each time.
