The substrate detail says a phenomenon hypothesis names its setup, either the reference setup or a controlled variant. It doesn't say what makes a variant legitimate. A phenomenon the domain only ever observes alongside some other component (a stand's biomass curve, always measured with a working decomposer community) has no source when it is read in a variant that strips that component out. A reading there tests the model's own explanation, not the domain's phenomenon. Such a hypothesis is blocked by the hypothesis that the component works, as a phenomenon is gated by its substrate. A setup qualification that can't be met otherwise swallows the hypothesis: the qualification gets read again and again, and the phenomenon never is.
