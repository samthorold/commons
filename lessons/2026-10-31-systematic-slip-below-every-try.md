The flake/regression rule has a middle it doesn't name: the same fixture failing the same check on a large share of tries, but not on every one. A strict act check (refuse the whole answer if a quote isn't in its span) met a model slip that recurred on one fixture in about a third to a half of attempts: the same pronoun cited in a span that has none. By the rule it isn't a regression (some tries pass), and treating it as flake used up every fresh re-read the brief allowed before a run came out supported.

That middle points at the act, not at more samples. It costs a paid retry each time it happens in use, and it should be handed to the owner as a question: loosen the check (drop the bad citation rather than refuse), or change the instructions. Re-drawing samples doesn't answer it.
