"Read the Origin, don't trust the claim" wasn't enough on the first run, two ways.

- **The Origin agreed with a wrong fix.** A SQLite Lesson's fix (rename the old table first, under `legacy_alter_table`) matched its Origin PR. Only SQLite's own docs showed that the order is the one they call incorrect. When a Lesson is about an external tool's behaviour, the Promoter checks the tool's documentation, not only the Origin. That is also what *domain claims come from sources* asks of the domain layer.
- **The Origin often doesn't hold the claim.** Lessons a sub-agent reported (a grind's traps) cite the PR it was working on, whose body says nothing about the trap. The evidence is then the mechanism itself (here, that worktrees share refs). The Skill could say so, and the Lesson format could ask an Origin to point where the claim is shown, when that place exists.
