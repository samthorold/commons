A grill on a build that hasn't started (no repo yet) should settle only the decisions the first slices depend on, and leave the rest to the slice that first touches them. Grilling the Stack and repo was welcome; by the time it reached how ad earnings are allocated to players and which store account publishes, the owner called it "very academic" with no repository yet. Those later questions had real answers, but each could be settled by the slice that needs it, with the facts that slice checks. The skill has no rule for when to stop walking the tree and hand over to slicing.
