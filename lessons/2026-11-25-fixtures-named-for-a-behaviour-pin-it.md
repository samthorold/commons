A test fixture named for a behaviour (a "starving" founder, an "energy-dying" world, an agent with no body) usually shows that behaviour only by accident of the current rates. When the substrate changes, the fixture silently gains or loses it: here a lone starving founder began to reproduce once it lived longer, a dying world recovered by its horizon, and bodies founded with no structure stopped paying upkeep and taking up nutrient once both scaled with structure. The tests that used them failed far from the cause, or passed while testing something else. A fixture pins in its setup the behaviour a test relies on (the founder cannot reproduce; the world's income is below its cost by construction), rather than getting it from the rates, and a substrate change lists the fixtures whose setup it had to change.
