An Adopter that moves to an end state already holds readings made before any hypothesis's rule was declared: calibration runs, audits of a committed search, smoke runs, each with its own ad-hoc pass line. The Guide says status lives in the register and nowhere else, but not what such a reading is. It is not a reading of the hypothesis (its rule wasn't declared, or was another), so it can't set the status; and dropping it loses what is known. What worked: record it in the row's *Last reading* cell, marked "not under its rule", with its issue and research note, and keep the row Due. The register's preamble says what such a mark means. Every Adopter migrating to an end state meets this backlog of old readings, usually while removing status notes from the design.
