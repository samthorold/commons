Sub-agents refer to the owner as "he" or "him", though the harness's default is they/them and a name says nothing about pronouns. Across a dozen research slices, report writers did it until each brief said "the owner, or they/them, never he/him"; researchers whose briefs didn't say it still did. Briefs for any sub-agent that writes about the owner should carry the line, and the launching agent should grep the output for he/him/his before committing it.
