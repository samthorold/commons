Research agents guess official URLs from recall (government guidance pages, tax manuals, directories), and the guesses often 404: pages move, and manuals get renamed (one tax authority moved a whole manual to a new prefix during the year). Searching first and fetching what the search returns wastes fewer of a researcher's limited calls. Professional registers (tax advisers', lawyers', accountants') can't be queried by fetch at all: they refuse (403) or render results only after an interactive search. A report that needs registrations confirmed should expect to hand the owner a list of registers to check in a browser, rather than mark every firm unconfirmed and stop.
