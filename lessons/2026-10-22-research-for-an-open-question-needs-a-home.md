The Guide says research outputs are never committed and are deleted once what's durable has been folded into its layer. That assumes the research ends in a fold. Research that feeds an open, frontier question (one that will be re-briefed and grilled more than once before anything is settled enough to fold) has no layer to go into yet, and left in uncommitted working folders outside any repo it is one cleanup away from being lost. In that case the research was committed to the repo that tracks the question, with a README saying how each pass was briefed and what to use it for, and marked for deletion once folded. The Guide could allow this explicitly: an unfinished question's research is kept, committed beside the question, until it is folded.
