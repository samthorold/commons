"Resolve on touch" doesn't say what to do with a Lesson whose `waiting:` condition is unmet, and the Promotion table has no "keep waiting" row. Two agents condensing Guides in parallel met such Lessons and did opposite things: one left its Lesson in the inbox untouched (it was addressed to the Guide only as the nearest one), the other promoted part of its Lesson anyway and readdressed the rest. The README should say whether an unmet `waiting:` holds a Lesson back from On touch, and if so that the touch only checks the Address still fits.
